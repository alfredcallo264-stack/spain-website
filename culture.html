Culture & Food

Spain’s identity lives in music, markets, late dinners, and flavors that feel like sunshine.

A country built on stories

From Roman roots to Moorish architecture and modern creativity, Spain is a living museum. And the best guided tours often start with a spoon—or a glass.

Paella

Rice, saffron, and coastal flavor.

Fun to know

Paella is traditionally cooked in a wide pan and shared family-style. Seafood or chicken? Both are iconic.

Churros

Crispy, sweet, best with chocolate.

Snack like a local

Sangria

Sunset in a glass.
